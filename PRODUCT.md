# VAYU CRM

VAYU CRM is the operating workspace for VAYU Limited's customer relationships, sales pipeline, project delivery, invoicing, service catalog, notifications, and imported business records.

The primary users are VAYU management and staff. They work in the product frequently, need fast scanning and trustworthy operational state, and must be able to create, inspect, update, and permanently delete the records they are authorized to manage.

The product is a single-workspace application. It preserves existing authentication, permissions, record relationships, activity tracking, email integrations, and deployment constraints. Imported systems are sources, not authentication authorities; credentials, password hashes, tokens, sessions, and IP history are never migrated.

The interface should feel like an executive control surface: confident, precise, dark by default, readable for sustained use, and distinctive without hiding familiar CRM affordances.
